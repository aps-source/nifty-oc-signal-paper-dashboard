Reads gamma_log.json — one entry per tick, logged by a separate task from the paper trader (gamma_tick.py), pushed to this same site. Net GEX = (ΣCE_OI×CE_gamma − ΣPE_OI×PE_gamma) × spot² × 0.01 × lot size (retail convention: negative = dealers net short gamma = hedging tends to amplify moves). Gamma wall = the strike with the largest OI×gamma (CE+PE). Kept for the last 90 days. Paper trade: 1 lot of the ATM strike, flat at day start, reversal closes+reopens, forced close at day end, new entries only 09:16–15:14 IST, no stop-loss or target — same mechanics as the main paper trader, just driven by the GEX rule above instead of the price/OI/IV signal. Reads futures_log.json — one entry per paper-trade tick (same 4-min cadence as the option-chain collector), logged by the main paper trader (paper_tick.py). Quadrant: price up + OI up = Long buildup (fresh longs), price down + OI up = Short buildup (fresh shorts), OI down on either side = unwinding/covering (existing positions closing, not fresh conviction) = Flat/weak. Threshold: both the price move and the OI move need to be ≥0.5% over the ~15-minute lookback, else classified Flat. Recommendation: Long buildup -> Call Buy, Short buildup -> Put Buy, else No Signal -- the same gate applied to new option entries, shown here for comparison. Kept for the last 90 days.
