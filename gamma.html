GEX / spot history

Reads gamma_log.json from this folder — one entry per tick, logged by a separate task from the paper trader (gamma_tick.py), pushed to this same site. Net GEX = (ΣCE_OI×CE_gamma − ΣPE_OI×PE_gamma) × spot² × 0.01 × lot size (retail convention: negative = dealers net short gamma = hedging tends to amplify moves). Gamma wall = the strike with the largest OI×gamma (CE+PE). Kept for the last 90 days. Paper trade: 1 lot of the ATM strike, flat at day start, reversal closes+reopens, forced close at day end, new entries only 09:16–15:14 IST, no stop-loss or target — same mechanics as the main paper trader, just driven by the GEX rule above instead of the price/OI/IV signal.
